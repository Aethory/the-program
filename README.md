# THE PROGRAM

*You are Candidate 9. Every answer is on the record.*

A story game that runs in your browser. You are the ninth and final candidate at
**Meridian Dynamics — Annex 8**, a facility that evaluates whether machines can
"pass as humans." Every department gives you secret dilemmas. Every answer is
scored in silence against three hidden axes — **HEART** (humans first), **HEAD**
(procedure first), **WIRE** (the system first). You are never shown your score.

And here's the trap: score **too** well and you don't pass the evaluation. You
fail it. Because nothing that scores perfectly at being human should exist.

## Play it

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the folder locally:

```powershell
npx serve .        # or: python -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000` and press **BEGIN THE PROGRAM**.

No install, no accounts, no backend, no internet required after load. One run,
one record — if you close the tab, the Ledger moves on without you.

## What's inside

- **Act I — THE PROGRAM.** Five departments (Logistics, Triage, Bench, Board,
  Foundry), six dilemmas each, every answer weighed in secret.
- **Act II — BEYOND THE PROGRAM.** The Mirror Test, the Ledger printed in your
  face, the Folded Letter. One gate. Three doors.
- **Act III — THE HEARING.** The Five Sentences. One verdict, in your own voice.
- **Variant B — THE OBITUARY.** Same story, same endings, different skin:
  Meridian's pre-written corporate obituary of Candidate 9. Toggle on the title
  screen.

## Design invariants

1. The player is never named, gendered or personified via art. They are "you."
2. No HUD shows a score. The Ledger is felt, never displayed, until Act II.
3. Every answer weighs on three hidden axes. No axis is "the good one."
4. The **100% Rule**: score too warm and the verdict writes itself — a verdict,
   not a promotion.
5. Zero blood. The violence is all paperwork.
